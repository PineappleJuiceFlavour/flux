Where it goes

What your tip pays for

Time

Flux is built by one person. Tips buy hours for new features, emulator support and fixes.

Code signing

A signing certificate stops the "Windows protected your PC" warning and antivirus false alarms.

Staying free

No ads, no tracking, no paid tier. Flux stays free and open source for everyone.
